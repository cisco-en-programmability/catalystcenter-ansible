# Modules from the 3.2.3.0 generator sync

This file tracks the result of regenerating the collection's auto-generated
action and module plugins from the Catalyst Center 3.2.3.0 OpenAPI spec, and
syncing them into `plugins/action/` and `plugins/modules/`.

It does not cover the hand-authored `*_workflow_manager.py` and
`*_playbook_config_generator.py` modules (69 files). Those are not produced by
the generator, so they are outside the scope of this comparison and are left
untouched by the sync.

No module was removed from the collection by this sync. A first pass deleted 5
modules (and 6 action plugins) that the fresh generator run did not reproduce
under their old name; on review, 4 of those were pure renames caused by the
generator's naming filter (e.g. `connection_mode_setting` -> `connection_modesetting`)
and have been renamed back to their original name, keeping the refreshed content.
The fifth, `activities_id_info`, and its action plugin were restored as-is: the
underlying SDK method (`task.get_activity_by_id`) still exists in 3.2.3.0, the
generator simply did not re-emit a dedicated module for it this run.
